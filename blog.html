THE LUXORA GUIDE

How to Care for Your Favourite Jewellery

Jewellery often becomes part of our everyday routines and special memories. A little attention to how you store and handle your pieces can help preserve their appearance over time.

Store Your Pieces Thoughtfully

Keep jewellery in a clean, dry place. Separate individual pieces where possible to reduce tangling and scratches. Soft pouches or a lined jewellery box can help keep items organised.

Be Mindful of Products and Moisture

Perfume, lotions and household cleaning products may affect certain jewellery materials. Follow the care guidance appropriate for each piece and remove jewellery when an activity could damage it.

Clean According to the Material

Different metals, gemstones and finishes need different care. Use only cleaning methods suitable for your jewellery, and ask a professional when you are unsure.

The best approach is gentle, regular care and appropriate storage between wears.

THE ART OF GIVING

Choosing Jewellery for Someone Special

A thoughtful jewellery gift can celebrate a birthday, mark an achievement or simply remind someone that they are appreciated. Choosing well begins with considering the person receiving it.

Think About Their Personal Style

Notice whether they usually wear delicate pieces, bold accessories, simple designs or more detailed jewellery. A gift that suits their preferences is more likely to become a favourite.

Consider the Occasion

A small everyday piece may suit a simple gesture, while a more distinctive design might be appropriate for a major celebration. The meaning behind the gift matters just as much as the occasion.

Choose with Care

Consider the materials, design and care requirements. If you are unsure what to choose, ask the recipient about their preferences or contact Luxora for help exploring suitable options.

EXPRESS YOURSELF

Finding Your Own Jewellery Style

Personal style is not about following every trend. It is about discovering the colours, shapes and designs that you enjoy and feel comfortable wearing.

Start with What You Love

Look at the jewellery you already enjoy wearing. You might be drawn to simple necklaces, statement earrings, rings or layered bracelets. These preferences can help guide your choices.

Experiment with Combinations

Try pairing different pieces and consider how they complement your outfits and the occasion. You can keep your look understated or explore more expressive combinations when you feel like it.

Let Your Style Evolve

Your preferences may change over time. Give yourself room to explore new designs while keeping the pieces that remain meaningful to you.

Ultimately, the most personal jewellery choices are the ones that feel right for you.
